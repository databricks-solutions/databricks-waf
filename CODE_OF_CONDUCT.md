# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in this project to be safe, constructive and useful. Be respectful, assume good
intent while staying specific about impact, welcome questions, and critique the work rather than the
person. Make space for people with different backgrounds, roles and levels of Databricks experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, publishing somebody else's private information, or using project
spaces for unrelated promotion.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments and other spaces
where somebody is representing this project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not open a public
issue containing sensitive personal information. Project maintainers will review reports in confidence,
take proportionate action and protect reporters from retaliation where possible.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not meet
this standard, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for repeated or serious conduct.
