# Changelog

This project follows [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). The methodology version is tracked
separately because changing assessment logic affects comparability independently of an application release.

## Unreleased

- Extend OE-02-02 and its IU-03-03 alias to classic job clusters in the live inventory. Assess
  all-purpose and job policy adoption separately and use the lower share for the outcome. Job
  definitions without a cluster in the inventory remain outside the measured population.
- Open Methodology Version 3 as a candidate for this scoring change. Versions 1 and 2 remain frozen;
  the product owner must approve the candidate before release.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-08-28

- Established the clean distribution baseline for the Databricks Well-Architected Framework assessment.
- Added the supported DAB install, upgrade, recovery, rollback and uninstall lifecycle.
- Added configurable assessment scope, automated indicative scores, guided review, immutable reports,
  improvement management, trends and specialist workload analysis.
